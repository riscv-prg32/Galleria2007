# Galleria 2007 — asset and rights manifest

Every asset family shipped in the cartridge, and every external source
consulted, is recorded here (implementation brief §33). The rule is simple:
**if rights are unclear, the source is `REFERENCE ONLY` and no bytes or
derivatives of it are shipped.** `tools/validate_assets.py` fails the build
when a family is missing from this file or when an unrecorded raster file
appears under `assets/`.

Status of version 0.1.0: **no photograph, scan or third-party artwork is
shipped.** All textures, sprites, music and sounds are generated by the
project's own deterministic tools.
