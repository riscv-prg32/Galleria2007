# Audio

All music and sound is original and generated by `tools/build_audio.py`.
The requested emotional references are translated into *functions*; no
melody, motif or style signature from any film or series is quoted or
imitated.

## Instruments (AUD0, procedural only)

The AUDIO block contains no PCM bytes: every instrument is a PRG32 SID-like
procedural voice encoded with the documented `sample_id` bit layout
(`0x8000 | resonance<<10 | cutoff<<6 | pulse_width<<2 | waveform`).

| Id | Name | Voice | Default pan | Use |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 0 | BASS | pulse, filtered | 0 | music |
| 1 | PAD | triangle | −28 | music, MEMORY melody |
| 2 | LEAD | saw, filtered | +24 | music |
| 3 | PERC | noise | +14 | music |
| 4 | CLICK | short pulse | — | torch, menus |
| 5 | STEP | low noise | — | footsteps, drops |
| 6 | CHIME | triangle | — | pickups, records |
| 7 | RUMBLE | noise, resonant low-pass | — | masonry, gates |
| 8 | DRIP | triangle | — | water drops (ambience) |
| 9 | ALERT | saw | — | LampMan sees you, capture |
| 10 | HEAVY | dark noise | — | LampMan's footsteps |

The PRG32 tracker plays `NOTE_ON` on channel *N* with instrument *N*, so
music uses voices 0–3; effects use voices 4 (ambience/steps) and 5 (events)
through `prg32_audio_note()`. When `prg32_audio_get_mode()` reports stereo
(the default PRG32 profiles run 8 voices), voice 6 carries LampMan and
distant events and voice 7 the drops and the second layer of the wall
rumble; in mono those sounds share voice 4, so the 6-voice mono baseline is
respected.

## Stereo

The soundtrack and the effects are stereo and mono-safe (in mono the
firmware accepts pan values and mixes to one output):

- **Music:** instruments sit at different default pans, and every track
  carries tracker `SET_PAN` moves (69 in total): the exploration lead answers
  itself left/right phrase by phrase, the mystery's glassy notes drift so
  their origin is uncertain, the threat ostinato's percussion alternates
  sides, the chase percussion ping-pongs every 8th note with stabs swapping
  sides, the discovery motif widens from centre to wide, and the memory theme
  stays close and nearly centred.
- **Positional effects:** `sfx_from()` (`src/game.c`) places a world sound
  with `pan = 63·x / (|x| + |z|)` from its camera-space position (x right,
  z forward) and a gain falling to zero at 25 m; `prg32_audio_set_channel_pan()`
  sets the pan used by the next `prg32_audio_note()`. Positional sounds:
  **LampMan's footsteps** (every ~85 cm he walks — you can hear which side he
  is on before you see his lamp), his alert, the wall rumble (with a
  mirrored second layer in stereo), forcing the masonry, gates and the grate,
  the cistern water, item pickups. Player footsteps alternate feet
  (−10/+10), the torch click sits on the torch hand (+14), water drops fall
  at random sides.

`tests/test_game.c` checks that a source on the right pans right, on the
left pans left, ahead stays centred, and beyond 25 m is silent. Listening on
two speakers is part of the hardware acceptance ([acceptance.md](acceptance.md)):
the QEMU firmware streams a single mixed channel.

Tracker timing follows the current PRG32 documentation: one tick is a 16th
note, `delta_ticks` is the wait *after* an event, and delta-0 events form a
chord. (Firmware built before PRG32 commit `cd7f705` charged a tick per event
and decoded synth ids differently; the music then sounds slower and more
muffled.)

## Adaptive states

| State | Track | Tempo | Function |
|---|---|---:|---|
| EXPLORATION | D-dorian theme, 8 bars | 84 | curiosity rather than triumph |
| DISCOVERY | one-shot rising motif | 96 | sparingly, for the cavity and the 75 steps |
| MYSTERY | low D pulse, tritone pad, sparse glassy notes | 70 | deeper zones, after the sighting |
| THREAT | sparse ostinato | 100 | LampMan alert / searching nearby |
| CHASE | driving pulse + percussion, 4 bars | 144 | brief, avoids fatigue |
| MEMORY | intimate triangle melody | 66 | the shelter, the end screens |
| SILENCE | no track | — | after the wall opens: environment only |

`choose_music()` (`src/game.c`) picks the state every tick; `audio_update()`
switches urgent states (CHASE, THREAT, DISCOVERY, SILENCE) at once and calm
changes on the next bar line, and never cuts the discovery motif short.

## Environment

Water drops at random intervals (more frequent near the cistern), footsteps
every ~75 cm, torch click, item chimes, masonry rumble, gate creak, LampMan
alert. Silence is a deliberate state.

Audio budget: 2,680 bytes (6 tracks, 11 instruments, 626 events including 69 pan moves).
